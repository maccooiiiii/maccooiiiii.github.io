(02) About

Quality is a habit, not an audit

My career started on factory floors, running HACCP and GMP audits. Since then I've built a habit of asking where an error came from before deciding how to fix it.

Over the past decade I've worked in quality assurance, technical escalations, recruitment and training, digital marketing and data. As a Senior Data Quality Analyst in logistics, I lead the work that keeps logistics data accurate, consistent and reliable across internal systems and external partners.

I studied computer science and spent six years as a freelance essay writer. That means I can work through a messy dataset and still write a report people will actually read.
